Switch layouts with Cmd+Shift

Tap Cmd+Shift and your keyboard layout switches, just like Punto Switcher on Windows. Shortcuts such as Cmd+Shift+Z are left alone.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Free · macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · v1.0.0

How to install

  1. 1

    Download LangSwitcher.dmg

    Click “Download for macOS”. LangSwitcher.dmg is saved to your Downloads folder.

    Downloads
    LangSwitcher.dmgv1.0.0
  2. 2

    Drag it to Applications

    Open the DMG and drag LangSwitcher onto the Applications folder.

    LangSwitcher
  3. 3

    First launch

    macOS warns that the app is from an unidentified developer, because it isn’t notarized yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.

    Privacy & Security

    Privacy & Security

    “LangSwitcher” was blocked to protect your Mac.

    Open Anyway
  4. 4

    Grant Input Monitoring

    Choose “Grant permission…” in the app’s menu, or turn LangSwitcher on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring.

    Privacy & Security

    Input Monitoring

    LangSwitcher
  5. 5

    You’re set

    The layout indicator (EN/RU) appears in the menu bar. Tap Cmd+Shift to switch, and optionally turn on “Launch at login” in its menu.
